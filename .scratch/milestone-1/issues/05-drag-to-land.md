# Drag-to-land valley and mountain Folds, matched to the target

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: task
Blocked by: 03, 04

One finger on the paper picks up the nearest corner or edge point; dragging shows the paper folding live; letting go is matched against the Step's target Fold (right flap, right direction, line within a forgiving tolerance). A match snaps to the exact Fold; a miss springs back calmly. Pencil works the same as one finger. Symmetric Folds in a Step in any order.
