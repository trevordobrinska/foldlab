# Model file and player, with saved progress

Status: resolved
Type: task
Blocked by: 03

The Model file: name, Source, Paper (shape, two sides), Steps of Folds as engine calls, plus FOLD frames from replay. A player that walks the Steps, and saves "finished" and "current Step" through `src/storage.ts` so an unfinished Model reopens where he left it.

- **The Model file** (`src/model/model.ts`: types and `parseModel`, no engine, safe for the page). `version`, `id` (lower-case, used in file names and saved progress), `name`, `source: { name }`, `paper: { width, height, front, back }` (two #rrggbb colours; `front` faces him at the start), and `steps`: each with `text`, `folds`, and `frame`, the Paper after the Step as a FOLD frame (crease pattern, fold angles, `faceOrders`, plus `foldlab:rootFace` and `foldlab:view`; lossless, `paperOf(frame)` gives the engine's Paper back). A Fold is `call` (the engine call: `{ type: "valley" | "mountain", line, flap, layers? }`, as he sees the Paper) and `recordings`: one Recording for every state of the Step the Fold can be made from, each tagged `after` (the Step's other Folds already made). The written order is always there; when a Step's Folds are symmetric every other order is too. `parseModel` lists every problem with its Step and Fold, and refuses a Step that can be begun in an order it can't be finished in.
- **Building one, for Import** (`src/model/build.ts`, never on the page): `buildModel(recipe)` takes name + Source + Paper + Steps of engine calls (`ModelRecipe`; it accepts `unknown` and checks it first with `parseRecipe`, since it will come from the Claude API), replays every Fold in the engine, records it, and returns `{ ok: true, model }` or why not: `invalid` (problems listed, engine never run), `refused` (Step and Fold numbered from 1, and the engine's refusal), or `unrecordable`. Those are a Failed import's reason. `serializeModel(model)` is the file's text. A Step of 2 to 4 Folds is also replayed in every other order; the extra Recordings are kept only if every order replays and leaves the Paper looking the same, otherwise the Step is written-order only. `npm run library` (`scripts/library.mjs`, like `npm run record`) writes `src/library/<id>.json` from the recipes.
- **The player** (`src/model/player.ts`, pure): a `Place` is the Step, the Folds of it made (in the order he made them) and `finished`. `opened`, `toMake` (the Folds on offer now, each with its Recording from the paper as it lies), `made`, `undoing` / `undone`, `steppedBack`, `startedAgain`, `shown` (what the paper shows at rest) and `progressOf`. Saved progress (`src/model/progress.ts`) is `{ step, finished }` under `foldlab:progress:<id>`, only through `src/storage.ts`; unreadable data or a browser that refuses to save never stops him folding.
- **On the page.** `src/library/library.ts` fetches a Model file lazily and checks it (`openModel`); `src/view/model-papers.ts` places every Recording of a Model about one centre, so the paper stays put between Folds, and fits the camera once; `src/player-page.ts` is the thin DOM layer (words, buttons, saving) and the Model's half of the gesture host (`folds`, `show`, `settle`, `folded`). `src/main.ts` only wires them. `steppedPaper` gained an optional centre and `PaperView` a `colours` method.
